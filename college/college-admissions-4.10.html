Why Civil Engineering Is the Optimal Selection for 4.10 GPA Candidates
At a 4.10 GPA, Mechanical Engineering admissions are intensely congested by aerospace and automotive applicant volume, creating single-digit admit rates (UCLA 3.9%, Cal Poly SLO ~5%, UC Berkeley ~7–9%). In contrast, Civil Engineering offers almost double the admission odds (UCLA 7.6%, Cal Poly SLO 12–15%, UC Davis 30–35%) while holding #1 national ranking prestige at UC Berkeley and Cal Poly SLO. Note: UC Santa Barbara and UC Riverside offer Mechanical but do not offer Civil; UC Santa Cruz offers neither.
