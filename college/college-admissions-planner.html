Strategic Takeaway
Why Civil Engineering Is the Optimal Selection for 4.10 GPA Candidates
Across both UC and CSU systems, Mechanical Engineering admissions are intensely congested by aerospace and automotive applicants, creating single-digit admit rates (e.g. UCLA 3.9%, Cal Poly SLO 5–8%, UC Berkeley 7–9%). In contrast, Civil Engineering offers almost double the admission odds (UCLA 7.6%, Cal Poly SLO 12–15%, UC Davis 30–35%) while holding #1 national ranking prestige at UC Berkeley and Cal Poly SLO.
