Important — modeling assumptions (Version 3.0):
Legislation: TCJA individual tax brackets (10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, 37%) are modeled as permanent per the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21). Brackets held constant (unindexed) for simplicity.
Strategies: "Target All-In Effective Rate" uses binary search to convert up to a combined marginal ceiling. "Fill Federal Tax Bracket" calculates dynamic annual conversion headroom to fill ordinary income up to the selected federal bracket ceiling.
Tax payment method: "Outside savings" — full IRA withdrawal enters Roth; tax paid separately (maximizes Roth growth). "From conversion (withheld)" — taxes are withheld directly from the IRA withdrawal, and only after-tax proceeds enter the Roth account.
Social Security: Gated by SS Start Age (benefits only factored when age ≥ SS Start Age). Taxability follows standard IRS provisional income formula (up to 85% taxable federally; California exempts SS entirely).
California State Tax: 2024 brackets up to 12.3% plus 1% Mental Health Services tax on taxable income over $1M. CA standard deduction ($11,080 MFJ / $5,540 Single) included.
NIIT (Net Investment Income Tax): IRS Form 8960 rule strictly enforced: 3.8% on the lesser of Net Investment Income or MAGI excess above threshold ($250K MFJ / $200K Single). If NII is $0, NIIT is $0.
IRMAA: Medicare Part B + D surcharges applied starting at Medicare age using the statutory 2-year lookback rule (age A surcharge based on age A-2 MAGI). Pre-plan baseline MAGI is used for early-year lookbacks.
This tool is for educational planning only and does not constitute financial, tax, or legal advice. Consult a licensed CPA and/or CFP before executing any conversion strategy.
