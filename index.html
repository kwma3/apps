Important — modeling assumptions: Federal brackets: 2024 MFJ/Single brackets held constant (real brackets inflation-adjust annually). CA tax: 2024 brackets up to 13.3% (1% mental health surtax on income >$1M included); CA does not tax Social Security income. Social Security: up to 85% of SS benefits included in federal taxable income (standard IRS formula); CA exempts SS entirely. NIIT: 3.8% on net investment income for MAGI above threshold (default $250K MFJ / $200K single). IRA conversions are ordinary income, not investment income — NIIT modeled here applies to existing investment income displaced into NIIT territory by the conversion-driven MAGI increase. IRMAA: Medicare Part B + D combined surcharges applied by MAGI tier starting at the IRMAA age you set. 2024 surcharge amounts used.
